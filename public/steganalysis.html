TECHNICAL NOTES

In a natural image, adjacent byte values (called PoV pairs: 0↔1, 2↔3, 4↔5...) occur with roughly equal frequency. Changing the LSB of a byte flips it between a pair. Sequential embedding balances these pairs artificially, creating a "flat" distribution that Chi-Square analysis detects immediately.

Shadow Fold uses a password-seeded Mersenne Twister to shuffle pixel channels before writing. This scatters the data across the entire image, ensuring no detectable boundary exists. The result is statistically indistinguishable from natural sensor noise.
